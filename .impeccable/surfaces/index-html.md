# Landing (index.html)

Scope: the public front door at `/`. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: invited learners holding the password. Action: sign in with the password to open the course at `/learn/`. Proof: real comic panels lettered with real phrases, one live card graded by the real grader with the recorded clip. Constraints: DESIGN.md world, copy rules, no invented claims.

Memorable moment: every drawn panel is a card. Clicking one sends its phrase into the live card.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first viewport is a comic page, and the product's title panel sits inside its grid. It refuses the category default: a split hero with a floating product screenshot and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: River primer: paper #f2ead8, ink #1c1710, one lacquer #9a2b1f. Every cell carries a 2px ink frame, with 12px gutters like a printed comic page. Brygada 1918 for headlines, Onest for the interface, Didact Gothic for romanization, Mali for the Thai in balloons, Fahkwang for the wordmark's Thai. All other colour comes from the drawings.

STORY: The visitor sees a day of speaking Thai drawn as a comic, and learns that the course teaches Thai in romanization, then the letters. They type one real card and hear it, then enter the password to open the course.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 64px nav: wordmark on the left; Try a card and a Sign in ghost button on the right. Below it, a 5-column grid of square cells. The 2x2 title panel sits at the left: a Brygada headline of about 4.4rem, a two-line Onest lede, and the password field with the lacquer Open the course button. That form is the primary action. The coffee panel is 2x2 beside it, with jasmine above door in column 5. The bottom row is mango, tea, stairs, umbrella and passenger. Balloons are lettered in Mali Thai. Panels ink in once, in reading order.

FORM: Comic page hero, first of seven in my ordered list, dealt by seed 0c1ea713 (surface, persuade).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
