# Batch 03: the flower redraw, and eleven cards for levels with one panel or none

Twelve cards from `art/shots.md`. They bring Voice 7, 8, 10, 15, 17, 18 and 23 to at least one
panel each, and most of them to two. Every prompt carries the lessons in STYLE.md under "Staging":
- the balloon is placed over whoever speaks;
- her T-shirt is named;
- every gesture has a direction;
- the manners are Thai.

## How to run it (Cursor)

- **Takes:** make three per card with the image tool, named `<stem>-a`, `<stem>-b`, `<stem>-c`. Twelve cards make 36 images.
- **Settings:** aspect 1:1, every reference image listed on the card attached, and the prompt pasted exactly as written, as one paragraph.
- **Don't change a word,** and don't use any other image as a reference.
- **Collect:** copy each image from `~/Downloads/cursor-generated-assets` into `art/candidates/` as `<stem>-<take>.png`. The flower redraw is named `flower-d`, `flower-e`, `flower-f`, so batch 02's takes stay.
