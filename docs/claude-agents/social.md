# Social subagent brief

## Role

Own friends, usernames, and shared progress decisions.

## Already decided

- Defer friends, usernames, and friends' progress until post-PMF.
- The validation product is solo signup plus the lesson loop.
- Social proof should come from real return use or paid users, not invented activity.

## Do not build yet

- Friend graph.
- Usernames.
- Shared progress views.
- Leaderboards or leagues.
- Invites based on friend activity.

## Open questions

- What social behavior learners actually ask for after solo use.
- Whether usernames are needed for support, referrals, or future friend features.

## Next concrete step

When reviewing any proposed feature, reject social scope unless it directly supports solo signup, the landing demo, or the paid lesson loop.
