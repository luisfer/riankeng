# Batch 01: calibration

Six cards from `art/shots.md`, chosen to test the staging rules on the hard cases:
- the toilet redraw;
- a counter;
- an outdoor wall with a child and a dog;
- a motorbike side-on;
- a child at eye level;
- two people sitting on a step.

What comes back sets how the other 44 are written.

## How to run it (Cursor)

- **Takes:** make three per card with the image tool, named `<stem>-a`, `<stem>-b`, `<stem>-c`.
- **Settings:** aspect 1:1, every reference image listed on the card attached, and the prompt pasted exactly as written, as one paragraph.
- **Don't change a word,** and don't use any other image as a reference.
- **When done:** the tool moves the files to `~/Downloads/cursor-generated-assets`; leave them there. Claude copies the newest into `art/candidates/`, grades them, and reviews every one before Luis sees them.
