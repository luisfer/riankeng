# Batch 02: the hero cards, and three more

Eight cards from `art/shots.md`:
- the five remaining ★ cards for the landing's rotation (cute, reheat, kitty, flower, shy);
- three simple cards for levels with no panel (come, time, hundred).

The prompts carry batch 01's lessons (STYLE.md, under "Staging"):
- the balloon is placed over whoever speaks;
- her T-shirt is named;
- every gesture has a direction.

## How to run it (Cursor)

- **Takes:** make three per card with the image tool, named `<stem>-a`, `<stem>-b`, `<stem>-c`.
- **Settings:** aspect 1:1, every reference image listed on the card attached, and the prompt pasted exactly as written, as one paragraph.
- **Don't change a word,** and don't use any other image as a reference.
- **Collect:** copy each image from `~/Downloads/cursor-generated-assets` into `art/candidates/` as `<stem>-<take>.png`.
