# The panels to draw

Fifty new panels and one redraw, for Luis to approve as text before anything is drawn.
Every line is a real Voice entry with a shipped clip (checked with `getEntry` and
`hasShippedClip`, 26 Sep). The people and places are in `art/UNIVERSE.md`; the staging
rules are in `art/STYLE.md` under "Staging". With these, every Voice level has at least
two panels, and Her day grows from 40 lettered panels to 90.

Each card:
- **Line:** the id, Thai and English.
- **Level and time:** the Voice level, and the time of day, which sets its place in Her day.
- **Place:** with the reference panels.
- **Blocking:** who stands in which third, facing whom, what the hands do.
- **Props:** what they rest on.
- **Speaker:** the balloon goes above them, tail to them.

★ marks a card for the landing's hero rotation. B1 marks the calibration batch, which is drawn first.
