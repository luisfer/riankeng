# Her world. The people she keeps meeting, and the places she keeps going.

The comic is one woman's days in Bangkok. She lives in a small building off a soi, and
most of her Thai happens within a few minutes' walk of her door. Every new panel happens to
someone we have already met, somewhere we have already been, or to someone new who then
stays. This file is the cast and the map. `art/STYLE.md` is the drawing recipe and the
staging rules; `art/shots.md` is the list of panels to draw.

Names are for us. The app never shows them.

## Her

Black hair in a low bun, one small gold hoop, a cream short-sleeve round-neck T-shirt, dark
navy trousers, flat black shoes. Dot eyes, a short mouth. A canvas tote bag when she is out.

- Reference: `art/refs/cast/her.png` (from coffee), and tea and coffee themselves.
- Some early and quiet panels give her a cream collared blouse (door, bike, stall, night,
  temple, canal, soi). New panels use the round-neck T-shirt, as tea and coffee do.
- Her trousers are navy. A few panels drew them black (help, pricey, pharmacy); new ones do not.

Rules of the cast:

- Each person plays their own role in their own place. Lung Chai stays at his counter and
  his restaurant: he does not sell fruit, carry luggage or guide anyone down a corridor.
  (He does in help, thanks and hotel; those shipped, and stay.)
- One of the cast, or one new person, per panel besides her. Never two lookalikes: a second
  woman never shares her bun, her hoop or her cream shirt.
- A newcomer who works out joins this table, with a crop in `refs/cast/`.

## The day's light

Cream paper in the day. A pale sky blue for mornings outdoors (jasmine), peach for evening
(pier, laugh), navy for night (tea, night, tasty, krathong), rain grey (umbrella). A quiet
panel paints its ground to every edge (see STYLE.md).
